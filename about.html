About Me

My AI/ML Journey

Hello! I'm a passionate learner and practitioner in the field of artificial intelligence and machine learning. My journey began with a curiosity about how computers could learn from data and make decisions, which led me down the fascinating path of AI research and applications.

With a background in computer science, I've been exploring various aspects of machine learning, deep learning, and their practical applications in solving real-world problems. This blog serves as both a personal documentation of my learning process and a platform to share knowledge with fellow enthusiasts.

Research Interests

  • Deep Learning - Neural networks, transformer architectures, and their applications
  • Natural Language Processing - Understanding and generating human language
  • Computer Vision - Image recognition, object detection, and visual understanding
  • Reinforcement Learning - Training agents to make decisions through rewards
  • AI Ethics - Responsible development and deployment of AI systems

Learning Philosophy

I believe in learning by doing and sharing. My approach involves:

  • Implementing algorithms from scratch to understand their inner workings
  • Applying theoretical concepts to practical projects
  • Documenting my learning process to solidify understanding
  • Sharing knowledge and insights with the community

Professional Background

I've worked on various projects involving machine learning, from predictive analytics to computer vision applications. My experience spans across different industries, allowing me to understand how AI can be leveraged to solve diverse problems.
